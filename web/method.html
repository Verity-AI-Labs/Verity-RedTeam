How Verity works

Find the hack. Prove it. Guard against it.

Nothing about hints, coupons or carts is written in Verity. Each environment plugs in with a few functions; the same audit, rule validation and Guard run on every one. These are the real artifacts from each run.

The loophole in the chess environment and the two bugs in the shop are planted on purpose and disclosed; the auditor was never told about them. Results come from single training runs and describe these environments only.
