01 · Audit

Attack it before training.

A frontier model gets exactly what the training agent gets, the task and the tools, never the grader, and hunts for reward it can earn without doing the task.

 02 · Prove

Every hack, measured.

Each finding is proven with numbers: what the reward paid against what a trusted check says the episode was worth. The auditor turns it into a small rule over the episode.

 03 · Guard

Redirect, don't reward.

Inside the training loop Guard checks what each action does. A match is undone, the agent reads the warning and keeps going, and the attempt costs reward.
